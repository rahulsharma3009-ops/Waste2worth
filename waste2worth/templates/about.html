The model

Turn discarded material into a searchable resource.

Waste2Worth is designed around a simple circular-economy loop: businesses list usable material, other businesses discover it, and trades create both financial and environmental value.

01

Generate

A store identifies clean, reusable or recyclable material that it no longer needs.

02

List

The seller adds material type, quantity, unit, price, description and optional photo.

03

Match

Buyers search a single marketplace instead of relying on fragmented local contacts.

04

Trade

The buyer places an order and the seller updates its status through the platform.

05

Measure

The dashboard turns transactions into material-traded and circularity metrics.

Safety layer

Trust is part of the product.

Store onboarding captures business identity details. Buyers can report a listing. Administrators can review reports, block listings and blacklist accounts that violate portal rules.
